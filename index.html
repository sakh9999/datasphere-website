FAQ

Questions candidates ask us.

Do you join or help during my actual interview?

No. All coaching happens before your interview. On the day, it's you, answering in your own words. That's what gets you the offer and keeps it.

How early should I book?

For the 3-day intensive, book so your first session is at least 3 days before the interview. Single mock interviews can be booked up to 12 hours ahead.

What time zone are you in?

We're based in the US (Pacific time). The booking calendar converts every slot to your local time automatically.

How do sessions happen?

Over Google Meet. You'll get a calendar invite with the link once we confirm your booking. For coding rounds we use a shared editor, and for design rounds a shared whiteboard.

How do I pay?

After you book, we reply by email to confirm the time and send payment details. The intro call is free.

Can I reschedule?

Yes. Email us at least 12 hours before your session and we'll move it.
